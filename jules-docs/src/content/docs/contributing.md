---
title: Contributing to Jules
description: Learn how you can help improve Jules.
---

Jules is continuously evolving, and your contributions can help make it even better.

## Providing Feedback

One of the most important ways you can contribute is by providing feedback on your interactions with Jules.

*   **Clarity of Communication:** Let Jules know if its explanations are unclear or if you have trouble understanding its responses.
*   **Accuracy of Information:** If Jules provides incorrect information or generates faulty code, please point it out.
*   **Usefulness of Suggestions:** Indicate whether Jules' suggestions were helpful and relevant to your task.
*   **Plan Feedback:** When Jules presents a plan, review it carefully. If you see areas for improvement, suggest changes. Approving well-defined plans helps Jules perform better.

## Suggesting Features

If you have ideas for new features or capabilities that would make Jules more helpful, please share them.

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter any bugs, errors, or unexpected behavior while interacting with Jules, report them. Provide as much detail as possible, including:

*   The task you were trying to accomplish.
*   The specific prompts or instructions you gave Jules.
*   The response or behavior you observed.
*   Any error messages you encountered.

Your feedback and contributions are invaluable in shaping the future of Jules and making it a more effective AI software engineering assistant.
